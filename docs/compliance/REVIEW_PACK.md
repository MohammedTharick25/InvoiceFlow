## Written calculation policy for review

1. Quantity is stored in integer milli-units. Gross line value is quantity-milli Ã— unit price in minor units, rounded half-up to paise by division by 1,000.
2. Line discounts are applied to that line. A fixed discount is a minor-unit amount; percentage discounts use basis points and half-up rounding. A discount greater than gross is rejected.
3. Invoice discount applies to item lines only. It is apportioned in proportion to each itemâ€™s post-line-discount gross using the largest-remainder method; ties go to earlier lines. Charges are outside this allocation.
4. Taxable value is post-discount value. Intra-state tax independently rounds the CGST half-rate half-up, then sets SGST/UTGST to that same rounded amount. Inter-state tax rounds the full rate half-up as IGST. `none` has no tax. Unsupported taxable lines for supply type `other` are rejected.
5. Exempt, nil-rated, zero-rated, and non-GST values remain separate taxability buckets. Their legal classification and use in a real supply require professional review.
6. Totals sum the calculated lines and charges. Integer minor units and bounded arithmetic are used; unsupported amounts and line/charge counts fail validation.
7. Tax rates are settings data with append-only versions. Seeded rates and HSN/SAC defaults are editable starting points, not authoritative reference data. Place of supply, tax point, taxability, and HSN/SAC selection are business inputs requiring review.

## Invoice numbering examples

Fiscal year starts in April by default. The fiscal-year label is the two-digit start year followed by the next year: 2026-04-01 through 2027-03-31 â†’ `2627`; 2027-04-01 â†’ `2728`. A January start uses the same `Y`/`Y+1` label convention (for 2026-06-15, `2627`), explicitly retained as a reviewer question. Prefix plus year, separator, and sequence must fit 16 characters; the default six-digit series is allocated only on finalization. Failed transactions roll back sequence allocation; cancelled numbers are not reused; exhausted series have no self-service continuation.

## Sign-off record

The reviewer should create `docs/compliance/ACCOUNTANT_SIGNOFF.md` only after completing the review. It must state reviewer name and qualification, date, scope, findings, resolutions, `calcVersion`, PDF template version, and template hash. Do not describe the product as certified. A changed calculation or PDF template requires a fresh review. Production release is gated on this record matching the code.
